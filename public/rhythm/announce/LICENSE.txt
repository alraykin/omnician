Speed Builder Announce voice clips
=================================

These short spoken-percent files (10–200) are used when Announce is on.
They play through Omnician’s Web Audio engine so timing stays with the
click or drums and no network call is needed.

Voice
-----
**Kokoro-82M** voice **af_heart** (American English, female)
https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M/blob/main/VOICES.md

- Model: Kokoro-82M v1.0 (82 million parameters)
- Voice pack: af_heart
- Sample rate of source render: 24,000 Hz

Rendered clips
--------------
Each file is `NNN.mp3` for integer percent NNN (010–200), spoken as
“{words} percent” (for example 085 = “eighty-five percent”).
Clips were silence-trimmed, loudness-normalized (about −17 LUFS,
true peak −1.5 dB), and encoded mono MP3 at 32 kbps.
