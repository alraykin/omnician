Click one-shots used in Omnician Rhythm (Click mode)

All sources are CC0 / public domain. Files here are trimmed, faded to a
short decay (~20–40 ms audible), low-passed (~3–5 kHz) for warmth, and
peak-normalized without clipping. Each voice has a tick (unaccented / soft)
and a brighter or harder accent (strong).
