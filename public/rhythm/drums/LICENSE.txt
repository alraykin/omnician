CrocellKit (main drum kit and extra cymbal lanes)

Omnician uses CrocellKit one-shots (5 intensity tiers, round-robin)
encoded to AAC 192 kbps for the web. Tiers keep their natural relative loudness.
Approved kick / snare / rimshot / toms / hats / crash / ride files are unchanged.
Ride bell, china, splash, and stack were added from the same stereo mix:
- Ride Bell — RideRBell
- China — ChinaR
- Splash — SplashL
- Stack — CrashRStopped (extra choked crash from the kit; CrocellKit has no stacked cymbal)
