Omnician uses the approved CrocellKit one-shots (5 intensity tiers, round-robin)
encoded to AAC 192 kbps for the web. Tiers keep their natural relative loudness.
Samples were edited before this pack (tightened toms, snappy snare); no extra
EQ or normalization is applied here.

Pieces: kick, snare (snare_snappy_p2), rimshot_snappy_p2 (hard snare backbeats),
sidestick (Rim), hihat closed/open, crash, ride, tom high/mid/low.
