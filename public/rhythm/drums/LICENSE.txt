Omnician uses CrocellKit one-shots (5 intensity tiers, round-robin)
encoded to AAC 192 kbps for the web. Tiers keep their natural relative loudness.
Approved kick / snare / rimshot / toms / hats / crash / ride files are unchanged.
Ride bell, china, splash, and catch were added from the same stereo mix:
- Ride Bell — RideRBell
- China — ChinaR
- Splash — SplashL
- Catch — CrashRStopped (caught/choked crash from the kit; CrocellKit has no stacked cymbal)

---

Drum-room send
A medium stereo impulse response is generated in the app (1.35 s decay,
15 ms predelay, damped highs). The return sits after the dry clipper at
about 1% wet (−40 dB vs dry). No third-party IR is used. Applied to drums
only; the click stays dry.
