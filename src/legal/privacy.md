# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 28, 2026
**Last updated:** September 28, 2026

Omnician is a music practice app made by **Raykin Music LLC**, a New York limited liability company based in Brooklyn, New York ("**Raykin Music**," "**we**," "**us**," or "**our**"). This Privacy Policy explains what information Omnician handles, why, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. It covers our website at **https://omnician.app**, our web app, and any future Omnician mobile apps, emails, and related services (together, the "**Service**").

We wrote this in plain English. The short version is below. The full details follow it.

---

## The short version

- **Right now, you do not need an account, and we do not run ads or analytics.** The free tools (Tuner, Rhythm, Listen) work without us knowing who you are.
- **Your microphone audio stays on your device.** When you use the Tuner or Listen, your browser analyzes the sound in real time. We do not record it, store it, or send it to our servers.
- **Audio files you open in Listen stay on your device too.** Your browser analyzes them locally. They are not uploaded.
- **Your settings are saved in your own browser** (in "local storage"), not on our servers. You can erase them at any time by clearing site data.
- **We never sell your personal information, and we don't share it for targeted advertising.**
- **We plan to add features** like accounts, practice plans, subscriptions, and mobile apps. This policy already explains how those will work. If we start collecting something new, we will update this policy first and, where the law requires, ask for your consent.
- **Kids:** Omnician is designed for general audiences. Children under 13 should only use features that collect personal information with a parent's or teacher's consent. See [Children and teens](#8-children-and-teens).

## 2. What the Service does today, and what we handle

### 2.1 Microphone (Tuner and Listen)

When you use the Tuner or Listen's microphone mode, your browser (or device) asks for permission to use your microphone. If you allow it:

- The audio is processed **in real time, on your device**, to work out pitch or tempo.
- The audio is **not recorded, not saved, and not sent** to us or anyone else.
- You can turn off microphone access at any time in your browser or device settings.

### 2.2 Audio files (Listen)

If you pick an audio file to detect its tempo, your browser reads and analyzes the file **on your device**. The file is not uploaded to our servers.

### 2.3 Settings saved on your device

To remember your preferences, Omnician saves some things in your browser's **local storage** on your device, for example:

- your selected instrument and tunings, and any custom tunings you create;
- your A4 reference pitch;
- Rhythm settings (tempo, click sound, drum style, mix) and other preferences;
- which microphone input you picked (stored as a device identifier your browser provides, so we can reopen the same microphone next time).

This information stays in your browser. We cannot see it. It is deleted if you clear your browser's site data for omnician.app (see [Section 10](#10-your-choices-and-how-to-delete-your-data)).

### 2.4 Information sent automatically when you visit

Like any website, when your device loads Omnician it sends standard technical information to the servers that deliver the site. That includes your **IP address**, browser type, device and operating system type, the page or file requested, the referring page, and the date and time.

- **Hosting and security (Cloudflare).** Omnician is hosted on Cloudflare's network (Cloudflare Workers). Cloudflare processes this technical information to deliver the site, keep it fast, and protect it against attacks and abuse. Cloudflare may keep limited logs for security and operations.
- **Fonts (Google Fonts).** Omnician currently loads a font from Google Fonts. When it does, your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser information. Google describes how it handles this data in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We may switch to hosting fonts ourselves in the future, in which case this connection will stop.

We use this technical information only to run, secure, and fix the Service. We do not use it to build profiles of you.

### 2.5 When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write. We use this only to respond, help you, and keep a record of the conversation.

### 2.6 Cookies and similar technologies

Today, Omnician **does not set advertising or tracking cookies**. We use browser local storage only to save your settings (see 2.3), which is strictly necessary for the features you ask for. Our hosting provider may use strictly necessary security measures to protect the site.

If we add anything that is not strictly necessary (for example, some kinds of analytics), we will update this policy and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent first.

## 3. Features we plan to add, and what they will collect

We are building more features. We describe them here so you know in advance how they are designed to work. **Until a feature launches, we don't collect the information listed for it.** When a feature launches, we will update this policy if anything differs from what is described here.

### 3.1 Accounts and onboarding

If you create an account, we will collect:

- **Account details:** email address, name or display name, password (stored only in securely hashed form) or a sign-in method you choose (for example, Sign in with Apple or Google, if offered). If you use a third-party sign-in, we receive basic profile information from that provider, such as your name and email.
- **Onboarding answers:** such as your instrument(s), skill level, practice goals, and how much time you have to practice.
- **Age or age range**, so we can apply the right protections for younger users (see Section 8).

### 3.2 Practice features

With an account, we will store information about your practice so we can build your plan and show your progress, such as: your daily practice plan, session times and durations (session timer), streaks, XP and achievements, Speed Builder tempos, drill results, and your settings. This lets your progress sync across devices.

### 3.3 Subscriptions and purchases

We plan to offer a paid subscription and content packs.

- **On the web**, payments will be handled by **Stripe**. You give your card details to Stripe, not to us. We receive limited information from Stripe, such as your name, email, billing country and postal code, the type of card and last four digits, and your purchase and subscription history.
- **In our mobile apps**, purchases will be handled by the **Apple App Store** or **Google Play**. Apple or Google processes your payment. We receive confirmation of what you bought and your subscription status, but not your payment card details.

We use this information to provide what you paid for, manage your subscription, prevent fraud, handle refunds, and meet tax and accounting requirements.

### 3.4 Possible future features

We may also add:

- **Recordings and videos you choose to make** (for example, camera-based "personal records"). If you record audio or video in Omnician, we will tell you clearly whether it stays on your device or is saved to your account. If it is saved to your account, we will store it so you can view it, and you can delete it. We will not use your videos for facial recognition, and we will not make them public unless you choose to share them.
- **YouTube slow-downer.** If we add a feature that plays YouTube videos, it will use YouTube's official services. YouTube and Google may then collect information according to their own policies: the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We will update this policy with more detail before launch.
- **Ear training and other drills.** We would store your results to track progress.
- **Artist Labs.** These are technique packs by guest artists. If you buy one, we may share limited, **aggregated or de-identified** sales information with the artist so we can pay their revenue share. We will not share your name or email with an artist without your permission.
- **Teacher dashboard.** A teacher (for example, a music teacher you invite or who invites you) may be able to see your practice progress, such as your plan, time practiced, streaks, and drill results. **A teacher will only see your progress if you (or, for a child, your parent) agree to connect with that teacher.** You can disconnect at any time.

### 3.5 Analytics (possible in the future)

We don't use analytics today. In the future we may add **privacy-friendly analytics**, such as **Cloudflare Web Analytics**. It measures things like page views and performance without cookies and without building profiles of individual visitors. We may also add crash and error reporting so we can fix bugs. If we add analytics or crash reporting, we will update this policy first, describe the tool, and ask for consent where the law requires it. We will not use advertising trackers or sell your data.

3. **Communicate with you:** answer support requests; send service messages (like receipts, trial reminders, and important changes); and, if you opt in, send practice reminders or news. You can unsubscribe from non-essential messages at any time.

We do **not** sell personal information, use it for targeted or cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

## 8. Children and teens

**Omnician is for general audiences.** It is not designed specifically for children under 13, but we know young musicians use practice tools, and some of our users are music students.

- **The free tools work without collecting children's personal information.** They need no account, the audio stays on the device, and settings stay in the browser.
- **Under 13 (United States, COPPA):** children under 13 may only create an account or use features that collect personal information **with the verifiable consent of a parent or legal guardian**, or, where the law allows, of a teacher or school acting for the parent in an educational setting. We collect only what the feature needs. We don't show children ads or use their information for advertising, and we don't let children make their information public.
- **Other countries:** if the law where you live sets a higher age for consenting to online services (for example, up to 16 in some EU countries), users below that age need a parent's consent.
- **Teens (13 to 17):** we don't sell teens' information or use it for targeted advertising. For New York users under 18, we process personal information only as strictly necessary to provide the Service you request, or with appropriate consent, as New York law requires.
- **Parents' rights:** a parent or guardian can ask to review their child's personal information, have it deleted, or stop further collection. Email **support@omnician.app** with the subject "Parent request." We will verify that you are the parent or guardian before acting.
- If we learn that we collected personal information from a child under 13 without proper consent, we will delete it promptly.

## 9. How long we keep information

- **Microphone audio and files opened in Listen:** we never have them. They are processed on your device only.
- **Settings in local storage:** they stay on your device until you clear them.
- **Technical logs** (hosting and security): kept for a short period by us and our providers for security and troubleshooting, then deleted or aggregated.
- **Account and practice data** (once available): kept while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your personal information within **30 days**, except for backups, which are overwritten on a rolling schedule, and information we must keep.
- **Purchase and billing records:** kept as long as tax and accounting laws require (generally up to 7 years).
- **Support emails:** kept as long as needed to help you and keep reasonable records.

- **Microphone and camera:** you can allow or block access at any time in your browser or device settings.

## 11. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- **Know and access** the personal information we hold about you, and get a copy (portability);
- **Correct** inaccurate information;
- **Delete** your information;
- **Object to or restrict** certain processing, and **withdraw consent**;
- **Opt out of the sale or sharing** of personal information, or of targeted advertising. (We don't do these, but you can still ask.)
- **Not be discriminated against** for exercising your rights.

**California (CCPA/CPRA):** California residents have the rights above. In the past 12 months, we collected the categories described in Section 2: identifiers (such as IP address), internet or network activity, and, if you contacted us, your contact details. We use them for the business purposes in Section 4. We have **not sold or shared** (for cross-context behavioral advertising) any personal information, including that of consumers under 16. We don't use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you.

**EEA / UK:** you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

**Other U.S. states:** residents of states with comprehensive privacy laws have similar rights. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision with the subject "Appeal."

**How to make a request:** email **support@omnician.app**. We'll respond within the time the law requires, usually within 30 to 45 days. We'll need to verify your identity, and if you have an account, we may ask you to confirm the request from your account email. You may use an authorized agent where the law allows. Because the free tools store settings only on your device, we usually hold no personal information about people who use Omnician without an account or without contacting us.
